Reasoning verified, not assumed — wavesurfer.js's Record plugin instantiates and owns its own MediaRecorder (we need control of mimeType and chunk cadence on the most latency-sensitive path), and no waveform library can visualise our playback at all because they all require a decodable blob with precomputed peaks, which a sentence-pipelined chunk queue never has. Build ONE visualiser component parameterised as {audioCtx, sourceNode} (the audioMotion-analyzer API shape) and point it at the mic MediaStreamSource when listening and at the playback GainNode when speaking — roughly 40 lines of analyser.getByteTimeDomainData + canvas, and one coherent visual language instead of two.
4. Make the citation layer the visual signature, using Tufte sidenotes rather than a chat-bubble popover. Retrieved passages go in the right margin at ~65ch measure using Tufte's label + hidden-checkbox + span pattern (pure CSS, collapses to a tap-toggle below ~1000px), with the source passage marked in Readwise's paper-marker yellow #FBDA83. Back it with the AI Elements 'Used {n} sources' Radix Collapsible for the full retrieval trace (BM25 vs dense scores, parent-child expansion). This is the highest-leverage move in the whole report: it is simultaneously the most distinctive layout decision available AND a direct demonstration of retrieval quality, which is the rubric's top-weighted criterion.
5. Lock the visual identity with four numbers, borrowed from verified sources rather than invented: (1) palette = tweakcn 'vintage-paper' — bg #f5f1e6, card #fffcf5, fg #4a3f35, muted-fg #7d6b56, border #dbd0ba, accent #a67c52, dark bg #2d2621 / fg #ece5d8 (note #a67c52 is only ~3.3:1 on the ground, so use it for borders and accents, never small body text); (2) --radius 0.25rem, not the 0.5–0.625rem shadcn default; (3) shadows with visible offset and low blur (2px/3px offset, 5px blur, 0.12 opacity) — soft 0-offset blurs are the SaaS tell; (4) serif in the primary slot (Literata) with mono for all metadata (chapter, page range, retrieval score, latency). Midday's shipped tokens independently confirm the formula: warm hue 40–45 at 5–11% saturation over near-neutrals, with saturated colour reserved for exactly one semantic role — for us, 'this came from the book'.
6. Two cheap polish wins and one thing to defer. Win 1: copy Streamdown's reveal technique by hand (~30 lines) — wrap only newly-arrived words in spans with a blurIn animation (opacity 0→1, filter blur(4px)→0, ~150ms, staggered delay) and strip the wrappers when the stream completes, so finished messages carry zero DOM overhead; in a paper theme this reads as ink settling. Win 2: copy AI Elements' three-state mic button (idle / recording with staggered pulse rings / processing spinner) and assistant-ui's action-bar rule (hide actions while streaming, auto-reveal on the last message, hover-reveal on older ones) — these are the details graders notice.
